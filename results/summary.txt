9. INTERPRETATION
The controller computes the state-feedback dot product using
a CKKS ciphertext and a plaintext gain vector. Only the resulting
control signal is decrypted before it is applied to the simulated
plant. The plaintext controller is retained as a baseline so the
effect of CKKS approximation can be quantified.
